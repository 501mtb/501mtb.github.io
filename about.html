01 / Identity

Born in Toamasina. Connected to the world.

501MTB brings together local roots and an international outlook. The name itself carries that identity.

What does 501 mean?

501 is the code of Toamasina. MTB stands for Mountain Biking. Together, 501MTB means Mountain Biking from Toamasina.

Read more
The number connects the project to its place of origin. The name keeps Toamasina at the heart of the identity while welcoming riders, partners and ideas from everywhere.

Local roots, international ambition

Our foundation is Toamasina. Our field of action and ambition can reach across Madagascar and beyond.

Read more
501MTB works to develop riders and mountain biking locally, while preparing people and projects to connect with the wider MTB world. International ambition does not mean forgetting where we started.
02 / History

Built step by step

501MTB evolved by strengthening its original community system, not by abandoning it.

2024–2025
  • Creation of the 501 VTT Facebook page.
  • Exploration of mountain bike routes around Toamasina.
  • Ride albums and a participation-based rider badge system.
  • First 501 XC Race project and community activities.
Read more
Each ride was documented in an album on the official Facebook page. The archive became a way to preserve the community's progress and recognize riders' participation.
2025–2026
  • 501 VTT becomes 501MTB, with an international-facing identity.
  • 501MTB jersey and stronger visual presentation.
  • 501 Riders 2K26 album and published ride calendar.
  • Workshop project, social activities and official website.
  • Rules, code of conduct and the system connected to the website.
Read more
The 2026 programme developed a regular rhythm of short, normal and long rides, depending on the Founder's availability. The second XC Race was prepared but cancelled because of Cyclone Gezani and road works.
03 / The 501MTB system

A system built around participation

Rides, events, archives and rider progression work together to build a stronger MTB community.

SHORT RIDE

Beginner-friendly distance and a first step into the community.

Read more
The short format is intended to make participation more accessible and help riders build confidence and experience.
NORMAL RIDE

The regular group ride format.

Read more
Normal rides help maintain group activity, develop consistency and bring members together on the trails.
LONG RIDE

A longer challenge for riders ready to go further.

Read more
Long rides encourage endurance, preparation and shared effort. The monthly programme is adapted to the Founder's availability.

Activity categories

Event, Major Event, Special Event and XC Race each have a distinct purpose in the 501MTB system.

Read more

Event: a standard ride, generally around 30 km and suitable for beginner-level participation.

Major Event: a longer, more advanced ride, generally around 80 km.

Special Event: a social, cultural or community activity such as a carnival or celebration.

XC Race: a cross-country MTB competition inspired by international race formats.

A living activity archive

Official rides and activities are recorded in albums on the 501MTB Facebook page.

Read more
The 501 Rider's albums use rider portraits and participation records. Activity entries identify the ride, date and distance, helping keep a traceable history of each rider's involvement.

05 / Open competition

Talent has no team boundary

501MTB develops its own riders and welcomes talent from beyond its team.

Open XC competition

The XC Race is designed to reveal champions, including riders from other teams and independent riders.

Read more
A rider does not need to belong to the 501MTB team to take part in an open 501MTB event. Participation connects that rider to the wider event community, without automatically granting an administrative club role or a badge.

Development is collective

Our ambition is to help mountain biking grow throughout Madagascar and prepare riders for international opportunities.

Read more
501MTB aims to train and encourage riders who may represent Madagascar through their performance. The project contributes to the wider MTB community; it does not claim to be a national federation or an official national-team selector.
06 / Governance

Two systems. One organization.

Field operations and external development require different skills, so 501MTB separates the responsibilities while keeping one overall direction.

Founder — Ni Aina

The Founder created the original 501MTB system and leads the organization's internal direction.

Read more
The Founder keeps the final decision for the overall organization. This foundational role protects continuity, the original system and the long-term direction of 501MTB.

Ride System

Responsible for the terrain, rides, riders, training, events, badges and internal organization.

Read more
The Ride System has its own team and focuses on the practical and sporting side of the community: planning rides, supporting riders and running MTB activities.

President System

Responsible for external communication, marketing, sponsors, partnerships and public representation.

Read more
The President leads a dedicated team for communication and external development. Members may propose and vote for a President when there are multiple candidates.

A one-season mandate

The President serves for one season (one year) and may be re-elected without a term limit.

Read more
The annual cycle encourages fresh ideas and renewed energy. Re-election remains possible, while the Founder retains final authority over the overall organization.
07 / Ambassador pathway

Contribute. Represent. Lead.

Ambassadors support official 501MTB activities and become part of the community's future leadership pool.

An Ambassador is a Super rider who sponsors a 501MTB activity.A pathway toward future leadership — not an automatic appointment as President.
Read more
The Ambassador badge recognizes a rider who has reached Super level and supports an official 501MTB activity. Ambassadors are considered part of the future pool of potential Presidents, while the President role remains subject to the community's candidate and voting process and the Founder's final authority.
08 / Vision & mission

A wider horizon for MTB in Madagascar

Our Vision

To become the leading reference for mountain biking in Madagascar.

Read more
We want to help create a visible, active and connected MTB culture, rooted in local communities and open to the world.

Our Mission

To develop and promote mountain biking by organizing events, supporting riders and building a strong and united MTB community.

Read more
